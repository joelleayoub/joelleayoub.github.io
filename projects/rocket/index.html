Firehorn 1 — Acoustic Levitation Payload

3U experimental payload on Switzerland's first cryogenic bi-liquid rocket. Custom KiCad PCB for avionics–camera communication. Competed at EuRoC 2025.

Overview

As Team Leader of the Payload Division of EPFL Rocket Team, I led the design, assembly and integration of a 3U experimental payload based on ultrasonic acoustic levitation. The rocket, Firehorn 1, became the first Swiss cryogenic bi-liquid rocket to successfully take flight.

PCB Design

Designed a custom PCB in KiCad for avionics–camera communication, selecting compact components and integrating power regulation to meet tight payload constraints (volume, mass, power budget).
