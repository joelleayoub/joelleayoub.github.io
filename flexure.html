Flexure-Guided Angular Inverter

Reduced angular error by 83% (3° → ±0.5°) through analytical dimensioning, COMSOL FEM, and laser-cut prototype validation. Instant-Lab, EPFL Neuchâtel.

Overview

Research internship at Instant-Lab, EPFL Neuchâtel. Designed and optimised a flexure-guided angular inverter: a compliant mechanism that transmits angular motion while inverting its sign, with high precision and low angular error.

Design Process

  • Analytical dimensioning: derived closed-form expressions for blade geometry vs. angular error
  • CAD modelling: parametric SolidWorks model for rapid iteration
  • COMSOL FEM: structural simulation to predict angular error across blade offset and orientation variants
  • Laser-cut prototypes: fabricated and tested multiple configurations
  • Camera-based measurement: experimental validation comparing optimised vs. non-optimised designs
