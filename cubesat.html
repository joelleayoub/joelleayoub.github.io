3U CubeSat Qualification Dispenser

Structural qualification dispenser for 3U CubeSat vibration testing: COMSOL FEM achieving first natural frequencies above 2000 Hz, exceeding ESA requirements.

Overview

Semester project within the EPFL Spacecraft Team Structures Division. Designing and manufacturing a structural qualification dispenser used in vibration (shaker-table) testing of 3U CubeSats before flight qualification.

FEM Simulation

Performed modal and structural FEM simulations in COMSOL to achieve first natural frequencies above 2000 Hz, exceeding ESA CubeSat standard requirements. Iterated the geometry to push resonant frequencies higher while minimising mass.

Mechanical Design

  • Redesigned mechanical interfaces for shaker-table compatibility and redundant load paths (two-screw minimum rule)
  • Corrected CubeSat rail tolerances to match 3U standard
  • Applied Type III hard anodizing for surface protection and wear resistance
