SAPIENS Humanoid Robot

EPFL's first full humanoid robot: built from scratch by a student team, with a redesigned dexterous hand and an imitation learning pipeline.

View on GitHub

Overview

SAPIENS is EPFL's first full humanoid robot, built from the ground up within the EPFL AI Team. The platform is based on Berkeley Humanoid Lite: a research-grade open-source design. As project lead I coordinate all subsystems, manage the budget and timeline, and drive the technical development.

My Contributions

Mechanical Assembly & Iteration

Led printing, iterating, and full assembly of the humanoid platform. The most significant modification was a redesigned dexterous hand with improved finger kinematics and contact surfaces for manipulation tasks.

Camera Integration: Hardware & URDF

Designed and integrated wrist-mounted camera supports both as physical mounts and within the robot's URDF model, keeping simulation and real-world perception consistent for the learning pipeline.

Imitation Learning Pipeline

Responsible for collecting and curating hand demonstration datasets using the onboard perception pipeline, for training manipulation policies via imitation learning.

  • System integration: coordinating mechanics, software, and electronics with 8 engineers in parallel requires careful interface definition

  • Demo data quality: designing manipulation tasks that produce demonstrations which generalise well for imitation learning
